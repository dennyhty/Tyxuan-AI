# Customer A is onboarded first

The first phase covers only Customer A. Its tracking lists record more than ten nodes per sample across five seasons, which a node-level Gantt needs, although its TPs are hand-annotated drawings that are harder to parse. Customer B has cleanly structured TPs but its tracking lists hold only start and finish dates, so it could support a total lead time and not a node schedule. Customer B comes second, to prove that adding a customer means adding a node mapping.
